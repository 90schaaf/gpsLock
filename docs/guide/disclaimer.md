## Safety first

- **Never** rely on GPS Lock in an emergency. Chaster's own emergency and unlock options always take precedence – make sure you
  know how to use them before you lock.
- **Never follow a rule into danger:** water, mountains, cliffs, ice, traffic, construction sites, private property,
  restricted or military areas, or anywhere you don't feel safe. Losing time on a lock is always better than getting hurt.
- Obey traffic rules and local laws. Don't use your phone while driving or crossing roads to check in.
- Don't let a lock push you into unsafe situations such as travelling alone at night, exhaustion, or ignoring weather.
- Keep your phone charged; an empty battery must never put you in a difficult position.

## GPS is not exact

Phone positions can be wrong by tens to hundreds of metres, can be delayed, and background tracking can stop without notice
(battery saving, missing permission, no network). GPS Lock tries to be fair (accuracy checks, confirmation times, hysteresis at
zone edges), but **wrong or missed penalties are possible**. Keyholders should be lenient when something looks like a GPS error.
